# Scenes

## Summer: couch-tv's scenes

`scene-*.jpg`, `scene-*-fg.webp` (the scenery cut out, drawn over the clock) and `scene-*-glass.jpg` (the clock's
fill) come from [couch-tv](https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv) by Seagey (MIT). Its author made them with an AI image
model from text only (not real places) and wrote: "Use them however you like."

`antonio-300.woff2` is Antonio Light (Vernon Adams, The Antonio Project Authors), under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(`OFL-Antonio.txt`).

## Effects

`fx-*` (rain, snow, petals, mist, stars, the Christmas fir's lights, baubles, tinsel and star, the Easter eggs and hare,
the moon and the bat) were drawn for Foyer with Pillow. `scene-fog*.autumn` are couch-tv's fog scene in autumn colors.
